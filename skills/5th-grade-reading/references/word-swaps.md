# Plain-word swaps

Hard words and phrases with plain ones that usually mean the same thing.
`scripts/readability.mjs` reads this table to flag them, so keep its shape: one
row per entry, the hard form on the left in lower case, the plain choices on the
right. A single word also matches its common endings (`utilize` finds
`utilized` and `utilizing`).

A swap is a hint, not a rule. Read the sentence first. Keep the hard word when
the plain one would change the meaning, or when it is the word your reader
knows from the screen, the law or their job. "(cut it)" means the words add
nothing, so delete them and check the sentence still reads.
